🌹 Discover Tyler, Texas

Website Planning Document

Site Plan

I chose this name because it clearly identifies the purpose of the website. Visitors will immediately know the site is a travel guide dedicated to Tyler, Texas, featuring local attractions, history, and restaurants.

Site Purpose

This website will serve as a travel guide for visitors interested in exploring Tyler, Texas. It will include information about the city's history, popular attractions, parks, museums, local restaurants, and travel recommendations to help visitors plan their trip.

Scenarios

  • What are the best attractions to visit in Tyler, Texas?
  • Where are the best local restaurants to eat?
  • What is Tyler known for?
  • Which attractions are family friendly?

Color Schema

  • Forest Green (#0B6E4F) - Header, navigation, buttons, footer
  • Cream (#F4F1DE) - Background color
  • Rose Red (#D62828) - Links, accents, highlights

Typography

  • Merriweather - Headings
  • Open Sans - Body text
